Our Story

The Artist Behind Every Stroke

Welcome to Mohan Mehandi Art — where traditional henna meets modern artistry. Based in the heart of Faridabad's Sector 15 Main Market, we have been beautifying brides, celebrating festivals, and creating memories that last a lifetime since 2012.

What started as a childhood passion for intricate patterns has evolved into a full-fledged mehndi studio trusted by hundreds of families across Faridabad, Delhi NCR, and beyond. Every design we create is a unique story — told through the language of henna.

We take pride in using 100% natural, chemical-free henna sourced directly from Rajasthan, ensuring the deepest color, longest lasting results, and complete safety for your skin.
