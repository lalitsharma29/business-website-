Pinterest API Connection

Connect your Pinterest account and automatically import Mehndi designs to your website

⚠️ Important: Pinterest API requires Developer Account Pinterest only allows apps that are registered in their Developer Program to access images. This is a one-time 5-minute setup — completely FREE. Follow the steps below carefully.
1Create a Pinterest Developer Account
  • Go to developers.pinterest.com
  • Click "My Apps" in the top menu
  • Sign in with your Pinterest account (or create one free)
  • Click "Create App"
  • Fill in:
    App Name: Mohan Mehandi Art
    Description: Display mehndi designs on my business website
    Website URL: your website URL
  • Accept terms & click Submit
💡 After creating the app, you will see an App ID and App Secret on the app dashboard page.
2Get Your Access Token
  • On your app page, click "Generate Access Token"
  • Select these permissions: boards:read and pins:read
  • Click Generate Token
  • Copy the long token that appears — it looks like: pina_ABC123xyz...
⚠️ Save this token safely — Pinterest only shows it once!
